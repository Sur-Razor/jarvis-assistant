JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Information Accessed

JARVIS accesses YouTube channel analytics only after the channel owner grants permission. This may include views, subscribers, audience information, estimated revenue, and video performance.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to generate private analytics reports for the channel owner.

Data Sharing

JARVIS does not sell personal information or share YouTube analytics with advertisers.

Data Security

OAuth credentials are stored as private GitHub repository secrets.

Data Deletion

Users can revoke JARVIS access through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the JARVIS project owner through GitHub.
